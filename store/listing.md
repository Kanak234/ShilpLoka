# Microsoft Store listing — ShilpLoka
**Category:** Games → Simulation (secondary: Educational)
**Short description:** Build ancient India block by block — Harappan bricks, barter trade and Vedic crafting.
**Description:** ShilpLoka is an offline voxel sandbox set in ancient India. Mine and place Harappan baked bricks, sandstone and banyan wood, trade spices with Dhanapati the merchant by barter, craft at the Vedic altar, and explore Harappa, Mohenjo-Daro, Lothal and Pataliputra. Heritage blocks such as the Ashoka Sthambha are protected. Your world saves itself every 30 seconds.
